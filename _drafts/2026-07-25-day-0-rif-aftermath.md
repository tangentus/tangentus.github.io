# Day 0

Today was my first day of being unemployed I got my restaurant job in college in 2010 or so. I was impacted by a RIF at the company that I was working for. **I wasn't good enough**. I'm comfortable with that. I can control that. Now I'm on the hunt for a Staff Software Engineer job in a tough market. B R I N G  I T.

I was blessed enough to have made it through several rounds of RIF events, always being on the other "employed" side of it. Anxiety over when the next RIF may be grows on you over time - it makes you question whether or not today is the last day or if tomorrow will be. For myself, it forced me to let go and let God. Thank the Lord that my identity isn't my career, or else "I wasn't good enough" would have been an anchor a the bottom of the ocean with my ego attached.

So here's the thing, I've talked my self through what it would look like to lose my job during a RIF (you know, since there were many of them), usually the Monday after I found out who was gone and it had sunk in a bit. I'd say things like "I'd get a job delivering Pizza immediately" or "I'd have 17 applications by midnight that day". Truth is, I didn't do either one of those. I prayed to God for humility and strength and I thanked Him for an amazing 5 years working on cool stuff. I thanked Him for the emergency fund that we built (shout out Dave Ramsey!!) that will get us by for a few months at least. After I prayed, I got back to work and finished like I started - curious, hungry for more work, and hoping to be a blessing as a team member and not a nuisance. The latter isn't up for me to decide, the rest I think I nailed.

How did my *actual* Day 0 go? 

**Surreal.** 

We just had a baby boy (Praise God!) who is struggling with lots of screaming fits - safe to say stress is at 1000% in the heat of the screaming. I don't think folks realize it, but when moments of stress and overwhelm hit you, subconsciously you iterate through the things in your life that you can count on. You know, like a paycheck! When that's not there as a backstop to your down-turned mood, you sink a little. But GOD! My wife and I have already seen His hand in things that we're excited about. Nervous, of course, but excited for seeing His plan unfold.  

**Focused**

There isn't any doubt in my mind of what has to happen. I need to pray and be in Gods word *often*. I need to act my wage (which currently is $0.00, so I'm acting broke!). I need to work like I'm on-call and own it all - 40 hours a week is a luxury that I can't afford right now. I need to love my Wife, because her safety and stability were rattled. I need to be honest with my sons and show them what it looks like to fail and get back up. I need to be honest with my community because in humility your in the best posture to witness God at work.

I focused on building an AI orchestration platform for home use. This enables me to work while I'm working. It also requires some forethought so that I don't blow through AI credits. I also focused on building personal projects that I've wrote in a note with all of my other "million dollar ideas" and relished in the idea of *actually* having time to see them through. I focused on finding metrics for my productivity and impact so that I know when to pick up the Uber Eats phone and start delivering. I focused on applying to jobs, not rushed or "just anything" jobs (not yet at least).

**Humbling**

Our best friends came into town this weekend which unexpectedly landed on my first weekend of unemployment. They've blessed our socks off with food, laughs, and even a date night with my wife. We ate BOGO ice cream from Publix and watched Youtube in our minivan for an hour or so. It was *awesome*. What spoons you ask? The ones we got from the soup area in Publix.

I'll be documenting my trials and errors through the job market as a Staff Software Engineer in this blog, as well as other posts with other content as well. My hope is that it encourages, brings light on the all-to-common RIF and its aftermath, and most of all I hope it sparks someones curiousity in God and the Son that died for mankind, Jesus Christ.

To God be the Glory, Amen!
